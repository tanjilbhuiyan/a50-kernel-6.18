4.14 uses `input/misc/hall*.c` and `motor/sec_vib.c` (gap matrix line 50: port small drivers or use mainline
equivalents). The vibrator's drive path (PWM, GPIO or a PMIC/haptic IC) is **unverified**; read it from the stock DT
before choosing a driver. Userspace expects whatever the stock vendor/Halium vibrator HAL uses (`docs/03-kernel-contract.md`
if covered; otherwise capture it first).
