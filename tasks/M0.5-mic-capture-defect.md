# M0.5 — Microphone captures no voice on the baseline (tracked defect)
Status: deferred — owner: UT-image session (not this repo's session) · Milestone: M0 · Needs phone: yes · Depends: M0.4 · Blocks: nothing in M1-M9; S6 microphone and call voice
Created 2026-11-02 (owner decision). Do NOT change mixer or PulseAudio state on the phone from this repo for this ticket.

## Why
S6 requires speaker + microphone. After the userspace fix (M0.4) the speaker works, but
the microphone returns only hiss and clicks. Upstream's own notes list "microphone
capture, calls" as never verified, so this may be an upstream defect and not a
kernel regression of the 4.14 baseline.

## Known evidence (log/2026-11-02-M0.4-audio-diagnosis.md)
- Recording analysed on the laptop (owner talking at the bottom edge, wakelock held, 12.86 s):
  floor -37 dBFS with 64% of energy above 8 kHz (hiss); the only loud event is a train of
  isolated clicks (~-16 dBFS, every 0.2-0.4 s, floor gated to -47 dBFS between them), no pitch.
  Earlier recordings are the same flat floor. No voice in the playback (owner).
- Kernel/HAL side appears active: `MAINMIC`/`SUBMIC`/`HEADSETMIC` DAPM pin switches on;
  dmesg `vogue_mainmic ev: 1/8` (mic-bias handler fires); `RECMIX1L/R BST3 Switch` goes
  off -> on during recording and Stereo1 ADC muxes match vendor path `dev-main-mic`;
  HAL applies `recording-mic` and `gain-recording-mic`; PulseAudio port `input-builtin_mic`;
  capture PCMs RUNNING (pcm9c, pcm11c, pcm12c).
- Fresh-boot recheck (2026-11-03 01:28, phone up 3 min after an owner restart, fix units active, wakelock held, owner counting aloud at the bottom edge for 13 s, "go" cue): 12.8 s at full rate, 612086/614400 samples non-zero, rms flat -36.8 dBFS (spread 2.8 dB), 64% of energy above 8 kHz (centroid ~12 kHz), 0 of 25 half-second windows voiced (pitch autocorrelation ~0.02), two isolated clicks only. A restart does not fix it, and the capture-stall seen earlier is not the cause.
- Owner reports (2026-11-03) hearing both sides in a call between the A50 and a second phone in the same room; same-room acoustics (each phone's mic picks up the other's speaker) make that inconclusive. Proposed: repeat in separate rooms as an end-to-end check. If the call uplink works while plain recording does not, the voice-call capture route differs from the `recording-mic` route: useful discriminator for whoever debugs it.
- Call uplink (2026-11-02 17:21): owner heard the other party, they could not hear the owner; voice PCMs and routes were active (pcm4p, pcm10c, pcm11c, UAIF0 SPK=1). Same symptom as the plain recording.
- Observed but unexplained: vendor path names `Main Mic Switch` which the kernel exposes as
  `MAINMIC Switch`; capture throughput once dropped to ~2% until PulseAudio was restarted.

## Candidate checks (for whoever takes it)
Other input ports (`input-back_mic`), codec gain/ADC volume controls, the other capture PCMs,
whether the mic bias regulator really powers up, analog vs digital mic routing for this board.
When testing: hold a wakelock, give the speaker a start cue, analyse the spectrum, not just non-zero samples.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A recording where speech is visible (level spread > 10 dB, voiced pitch 80-300 Hz) and audible on playback.
- [ ] Root cause category recorded (kernel / HAL / config).
- [ ] If the fix is userspace config, add it to the restore procedure in `docs/02-baseline.md`.
