# M2.3 — DTB/DTBO partition images in stock dt_table format
Status: done (log/2026-11-02-M2.3-dt-images.md) — S-Boot acceptance still unverified until M2.4 · Milestone: M2 · Needs phone: no · Depends: M2.1

## Why
S-Boot loads the SoC DTB from the DTB partition and merges DTBO entry idx 5.
Our 6.18 DT must be delivered in the same container format, or S-Boot will
not boot it (risk R9).

## Inputs
- Stock `reference/stock-images/dt.img` (`59209aa5…`) and `dtbo.img` (`40e47ed1…`), Android dt_table magic `d7b7ab1e`.
- Board selection: `androidboot.revision=6`, `androidboot.dtbo_idx=5`.

## Steps
1. Write `tools/dt_table.py`: parse stock images → list entries (id, rev, custom fields, sizes); dump each FDT.
2. Decide layout: (a) our full DTB in DTB partition + empty/no-op overlay at idx 5, or (b) SoC DTB + board overlay — choose the one S-Boot accepts with least change; document why.
3. Generate `dt.img`/`dtbo.img` with identical table fields to stock where S-Boot selects on them.
4. Round-trip test: tool regenerates stock images byte-identically from their parsed form.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] `tools/dt_table.py` reproduces stock dt.img and dtbo.img byte-identically.
- [x] Our images produced with matching selection fields; sizes ≤ 8 MiB each.
- [x] Rollback procedure (write stock images back via TWRP dd) documented in the log.
- [x] `log/<date>-M2.3-dt-images.md`.

## Evidence
`log/2026-11-02-M2.3-dt-images.md`, `tools/dt_table.py`, `reference/dt-6.18/images/` (gitignored images + SHA256SUMS). Stock images also carry a Samsung signature block + AVB vbmeta/footer after the table; carried over unchanged (stale) — see log.
