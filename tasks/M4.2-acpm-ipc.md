# M4.2 — ACPM firmware IPC for Exynos 9610
Status: in-progress · Milestone: M4 · Needs phone: yes · Depends: M3

## Why
On this phone PMIC access and CPU DVFS go through the ACPM power firmware, so M4.3 and M4.7 both need it.

## Facts / unknowns
- Proven on 6.18 by the M3.6 read-only probe (`reference/m36-usb/a50-acpm-probe.c`, `log/2026-11-08-M3.6-usb.md` Test 4): firmware answers; framework at SRAM + 0x6f00, ipc_channels=0x5ad8, num=15, ap_max=8; channel 2 (PMIC) polled, queue length 3.
- Stock DT: `acpm_ipc` mailbox 0x11900000, SRAM 0x02039000, IRQ SPI 0x26, `initdata-base` 0x6f00 (`reference/stock-dt/dt.dtb.0.dts:3032`). Channels: PMIC 2, DVFS 5.
- Mainline has `drivers/firmware/samsung/exynos-acpm.c` for gs101 only (mailbox plus `sec-acpm` PMIC client). Whether its protocol fits 9610 (4.14: `drivers/soc/samsung/acpm/acpm_ipc.c`) is UNVERIFIED: choose extend vs a small 9610 driver after comparing them.

## Steps
1. Compare the gs101 protocol with 4.14 `acpm_ipc.c` / `acpm_mfd.c`; write the decision into the log.
2. Add 9610 support with DT nodes (IRQ-driven or polled, per channel `ap_poll`).
3. Replace the test probe with a real client call: read-only PMIC register reads, compared against the baseline values.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] ACPM driver probes on 6.18 from the DT.
- [ ] LDO12/13/14 control registers read back through the driver match `0xcc`/`0xec`/`0xf0` (baseline, M3.6 Test 4).
- [ ] No firmware timeouts over 1000 repeated reads.
- [ ] `log/<date>-M4.2-acpm.md`.
