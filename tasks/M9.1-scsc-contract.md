`/dev/scsc_*`, `/dev/mx_*`, WLAN netdevs and `/dev/scsc_h4_0`. See
`docs/03-kernel-contract.md` and the Wi-Fi/BT row in `docs/04-gap-matrix.md`.
This ticket is preparatory; no M9 device work started during M8.5.
