# M10.1 — Audio source, firmware and consumer contract
Status: done (log/2026-11-20-M10.1-audio-contract.md) · Milestone: M10 · Needs phone: yes (read-only) · Depends: M9

## Scope
Establish the stock audio contract before selecting the port boundary, as M9.1 did for SCSC. Stock DT and config show
four parts: the ABOX audio DSP (`samsung,abox` + rdma×8, wdma×5, uaif×4, vss, synchronized-ipc, spdy, abox_gic,
effect, dsif, debug, abox-bt, `exynos,abox_rmem`), the Realtek RT5665 codec (`realtek,rt5665`), the NXP TFA9872
speaker amplifier (`nxp,tfa98xx`) and the A50 machine driver (`samsung,exynos9610-audio`, `SND_SOC_SAMSUNG_VOGUE_RT5665`).
Consumers: the 32-bit Android `audio.primary` HAL (PCM/CTL ioctls, compat; `docs/03-kernel-contract.md`), PulseAudio
(ADR-0005 overlay today) and the M5 `a50-audio-route` unit.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Record DT nodes, sources/config, buses (I2C/SPI), IRQs, memory, clocks/power and firmware for ABOX, RT5665,
  TFA9872 and the machine driver; note which have mainline 6.18 drivers.
- [x] Record firmware paths/hashes and the stock runtime: ALSA cards/PCMs/controls, mixer paths used by the HAL.
- [x] Capture consumer needs (HAL ioctls incl. 32-bit compat, PulseAudio modules, `a50-audio-route`), without
  changing mixer or PulseAudio state (ADR-0005; M0.5 rule).
- [x] Write the port boundary (modern-first) and the boot-test plan, with unresolved dependencies explicit.

Stock baseline limits to carry: playback works after the M0.4 userspace fix; plain microphone recording returns hiss
on stock (M0.5, UT-image session) while call uplink works. S6 on 6.18 is judged against that baseline.
