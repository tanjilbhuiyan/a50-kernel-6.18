# M3.7 — Halium initramfs on 6.18: mount /data, telnet over USB (M3 DoD)
Status: done (2026-11-10, with the caveat below) · Milestone: M3 · Needs phone: yes · Depends: M3.5, M3.6

## Why
M3 Definition of Done (docs/05-plan.md): Halium initramfs on 6.18 mounts `/data` and exposes telnet 192.168.2.15 over USB.

## Open observation to resolve first (from M3.4)
In the M3.4 test boot 6.18 logged "Initramfs unpacking failed: invalid magic at start of compressed archive" for the
bootloader-passed initrd (the boot.img ramdisk); absent in M3.2/M3.3 (log/2026-11-07-M3.4-pinctrl.md). The Halium initramfs IS
that ramdisk, so confirm 6.18 receives it intact (initrd address/size from /proc/device-tree/chosen, a marker file) before M3.7.

## Steps
1. Halium initramfs (baseline ramdisk contents) embedded in the 6.18 kernel image; size budget per docs/07-risks.md R1. Changed 2026-11-10: a ramdisk in boot.img does not reach 6.18 (absent at 0x89000000 at kernel start, log/2026-11-10-M3.7-halium-initramfs.md). Why S-Boot does not deliver it is an open observation, not needed for M3.7.
2. First run mounts `/data` read-only; then read-write only after a review in the log (it holds ubuntu.img).
3. telnet from the laptop over RNDIS.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] `/data` mounted on 6.18 (listing of ubuntu.img read back): read-only, 2026-11-10.
- [x] telnet 192.168.2.15 reaches a shell on the 6.18 kernel (`uname -r` = 6.18.x): 2026-11-10, from our diagnostic init.
- [x] Phone restored; `log/2026-11-10-M3.7-halium-initramfs.md`; M3 DoD evaluated in STATUS.md.

## Result and caveat
Halium ramdisk contents are embedded in the kernel (all 305 files verified on the phone), `/userdata` mounts read-only (`ro,noload`) with the `A50_UFS_SAFE` guard on, and telnet at 192.168.2.15 reaches a shell over RNDIS.
**Caveat (owner decision, 2026-11-10):** `/userdata` and telnet were set up by our diagnostic init (`rdinit=/a50/init`). Halium's own `/init` has not run: it runs `e2fsck -y` on userdata and may run `resize2fs`, so it needs deliberate write access. That moves to M5.

## Open observations (not blocking)
Why S-Boot's ramdisk does not reach 6.18: `docs/08-open-questions.md` #1.
