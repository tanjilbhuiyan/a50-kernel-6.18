# M3.3 — Exynos 9610 clock driver (CMUs for UFS and USB)
Status: done (log/2026-11-07-M3.3-clocks.md) · Milestone: M3 · Needs phone: no (build) / yes (test) · Depends: M3.1, M3.2

## Why
UFS and USB need their clocks. 6.18 has no exynos9610 clock driver (gap matrix docs/04-gap-matrix.md:25; the MCT panic of
M2.4 Test 16 is the same gap).

## Facts / unknowns
- Gap matrix: partial series on LKML ("Chimac v3, Jul 2026", excludes SHUB/MIF/ISP/VIPX/MFC); clk-exynos850.c as template.
  Availability and applicability to android17-6.18 are UNVERIFIED.
- Which CMUs feed UFS and USB on 9610: UNVERIFIED (read the stock DT `clocks` of the ufs and usb nodes and the 4.14 driver).

## Steps
1. Find and fetch the LKML series; record message-ids and versions in the log.
2. Map the ufs/usb clock phandles of the stock DT to CMU blocks; list the minimum CMUs.
3. Apply/adapt on a new branch (not a50-diag); add the CMU nodes to our DT.
4. Boot with M3.2 readout; check the CMUs register and that nothing that the bootloader left running is gated off (clk_ignore_unused at first).
