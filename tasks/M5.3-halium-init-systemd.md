# M5.3 — Halium's own init boots ubuntu.img to systemd
Status: in progress (2026-11-10) · Milestone: M5 · Needs phone: yes · Depends: M5.1, M5.2

## Why
M5 DoD: systemd reaches multi-user. This also resolves the M3 caveat (our diagnostic init stood in for Halium's `/init`).

## Facts / unknowns
- The Halium ramdisk is embedded in the kernel (M3.7). Today `rdinit=/a50/init` runs our init; dropping that runs Halium's `/init`.
- Halium's `/init` sources `scripts/halium`: `e2fsck -y` and `resize_userdata_if_needed` on userdata (`log/2026-11-10-M3.7-halium-initramfs.md`), then mounts `ubuntu.img` and switches root.
- Readout after boot: the DSS dump still works; SSH needs M5.4.

## Steps
1. Review `scripts/halium` and `init` in the ramdisk for anything that writes outside userdata; record the result.
2. Boot with Halium's `/init` and the M5.2 guard; capture the log through the timed dump.
3. Fix one blocker per boot until systemd reaches multi-user.
