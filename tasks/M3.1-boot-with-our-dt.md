# M3.1 — Boot 6.18 with our own DTB/DTBO
Status: todo · Milestone: M3 · Needs phone: yes · Depends: M2.3, M2.4

## Why
M2.4 booted with the STOCK DTB/DTBO, which makes 6.18 panic in exynos_mct (missing exynos9610 clock provider) and only worked
with a diag switch (log/2026-11-04-M2.4-diag-build.md, Tests 16-17). Every later device (UFS, USB) is added to OUR DT.

## Facts to respect
- Our DT has no MCT node (reference/dt-6.18/exynos9610-a50.dtb.dts; `grep -c mct` = 0).
- Our DT has no debug_snapshot reservation. The readout (M2.4 Test 19) writes the DSS header 0xf9000000 (64 KiB) and log_kernel
  0xf9010000 (2 MiB) (stock DT reference/stock-dt/dt.dtb.0.dts:1648-1655). Without a reservation 6.18 may hand these pages out.
- Our ramoops@0x91388000 placement is unproven (log/2026-11-04-M1.5-ramoops.md CORRECTION); pstore is not the readout any more.
- S-Boot checks `pit_check_signature (DTB)` / `(DTBO)` (reference/sboot/2026-11-07-sboot-good-boot-excerpt.txt). Our dt.img keeps
  the stale Samsung signature block (M2.3); whether S-Boot boots an "invalid" DTB is UNVERIFIED (BOOT "invalid" is tolerated).

## Steps
1. DT: add reserved-memory `no-map` nodes for 0xf9000000+0x10000 and 0xf9010000+0x200000; remove the ramoops node (or move it,
   decided in the log with evidence). Validate (dtc, dt-validate with a negative test), rebuild dt.img/dtbo.img with tools/dt_table.py.
2. Kernel: diag dump kernel WITHOUT A50_DIAG_SKIP_MCT (MCT has no node in our DT).
3. Pre-flight (tools/m24_preflight.sh) and rollback images as in M2.4 (stock dt.img 59209aa5..., dtbo.img 40e47ed1..., baseline boot).
4. Flash DTB + DTBO + BOOT with Heimdall (method A, one session). Software reboot path where possible.
5. Readout via DSS; then roll back DTB/DTBO/BOOT and verify with the pre-flight.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] S-Boot's verdict on our DTB/DTBO recorded (boots / refuses; signature line if readable).
- [ ] 6.18 boot log read back with our DT: `Machine model` from our DT, no mct line, the DSS regions reported as reserved.
- [ ] Phone restored (stock DTB/DTBO, baseline BOOT) and pre-flight passes.
- [ ] `log/<date>-M3.1-our-dt.md`.
