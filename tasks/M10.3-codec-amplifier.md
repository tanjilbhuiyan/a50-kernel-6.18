# M10.3 — RT5665 codec, TFA9872 amplifier and the A50 machine driver
Status: in-progress · Milestone: M10 · Needs phone: yes · Depends: M10.2

## Scope
Bring up the RT5665 codec (mainline driver if it fits), the TFA9872 speaker amplifier and the A50 machine driver on
top of the M10.2 ABOX core, with the stock DAPM/mixer paths the HAL expects.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Codec and amplifier probe on their buses; machine card complete; mixer controls match the HAL's paths.
- [ ] Speaker playback audible (owner); headset/microphone paths recorded against the M0.5 stock baseline.
- [ ] Stock restore/fsck clean; regressions pass.
