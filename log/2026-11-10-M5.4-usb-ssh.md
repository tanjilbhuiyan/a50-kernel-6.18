Later live SSH inspection (force-dhcp-inspect.txt) confirms systemd degraded
and the seven known hardware failures, plus usb-moded-udhcpd failed. Its log:
"can't bind to interface usb0: Protocol not available". Legacy usb-tethering's
ISC dhcpd is listening on UDP 67, explaining why host DHCP/SSH worked anyway.
Do not attribute the Busybox error to a port conflict: its actual failure is
SO_BINDTODEVICE, before address binding.

test config has NETDEVICES disabled even though USB RNDIS can build without
it. This matches the deployed udhcpd error exactly. No userspace daemon patch
needed. Test 2 adds NETDEVICES to tools/m51/gen_halium_flags.py prerequisites,
regenerated halium.flags differs by only -e NETDEVICES. Full resolved config
delta saved as netdevices-config-delta.json: NETDEVICES n->y, newly visible
network menus/defaults, and PPS/PTP defaults becoming enabled. No UFS guard,
