## D4a change and build

- **The single variable compared with D3b:** kernel commit `6c1151fb6` on `a50-m72-d4a-test`
  ([patch](../reference/m72-display/0011-TEST-ONLY-a50-decon-blanks-and-unblanks-the-panel-wit.patch)). The DT is unchanged
  (`src/m72/dt-d3a.img`).
- **Blank:** any `FB_BLANK_*` except UNBLANK, and power modes OFF/DOZE_SUSPEND. Sends the 4.14 `ea8076_exit()` sequence
  over DSI: DCS `0x28` display off, 20 ms, `0x10` sleep in, 120 ms.
- **Unblank:** `0x11` sleep out, 120 ms, resend the current frame, `0x29` display on (`ea8076_init`/`ea8076_displayon`).
- **DCS transport:** short writes go through `DSIM_PKTHDR` with the header-FIFO full/empty checks of `dsim_write_data()`.
  S-Boot left `ESCMODE.CMD_LPDT` clear, so commands go in HS.
- **Untouched:** the panel's power GPIOs and the manufacturer register set.
- **Config and initramfs** are byte-identical to M6.4 test 7. The guard and sense host tests pass.
- **Image:** BOOT `src/m72/boot-d4a.img`, sha256 `b531207c55e9ef2a9a6069177dc4a59adc3ef50f328b1f73fa07c1327444b449`
  ([inputs](../reference/m72-display/d4a-inputs.json)).
- **Test:** `tools/m72/blank_test.c` draws the colour bars, then cycles blank/unblank through `FBIOBLANK` and through
  `S3CFB_POWER_MODE`, counting vsync for one second in each state.

**M7.2 is done** with these limits, which are carried into the logs and STATUS:
- **Handover design:** the driver adopts the DECON/DSIM/panel state S-Boot leaves. It doesn't initialise the DSI PHY,
  link or panel from scratch, and doesn't power-cycle the panel through its GPIOs (full panel init sequence not ported).
  A display reinit after the panel loses power belongs to power management (M12).
- **Scanout:** one window (IDMA0/window 3) for full-screen 8888 buffers. Other layer configurations fall back to a CPU
  copy. Multi-window DPP, scaling and YUV aren't ported. Whether the stock composer needs them shows in M7.5.
- **Source:** all display commits are on test branches (`a50-m72-*`, latest `a50-m72-d4a-test`, `6c1151fb6`), together
  with the M6 test changes and the diagnostic kernel. The pinctrl fix (`cd08e9304`) is a real driver fix. Moving the
  M6/M7 kernel work to the clean stack and `patches/` is still to do, before the M7.5 soak.
