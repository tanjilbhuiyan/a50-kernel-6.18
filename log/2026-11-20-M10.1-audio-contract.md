Host-only analysis, then one read-only root snapshot on stock (owner present: "ok, continue"). No flash, no mixer,
PulseAudio or EFS changes. Tools `tools/m10/collect_audio_contract.sh`, `tools/m10/run_audio_contract.sh`; evidence
`reference/m10-audio/host-contract.md`, `stock-snapshot.txt`, `vendor-etc/`.

Results: four parts (ABOX DSP, RT5665 codec, TFA9872 amp, vogue machine driver). 6.18 already has the ABOX clocks,
reserves the ABOX memory and runs the same SysMMU type; ABOX's Samsung-only dependencies map to mainline APIs or are
dropped/stubbed (PM QoS, ION mmapfd, VSS until M11, SCO mailbox). The HAL's 146 mixer controls are covered by mainline
`rt5665.c` except `Disable NG2`, so the codec goes mainline. TFA9872 has no mainline driver; the stock driver reads and
writes calibration in EFS from the kernel, which the port drops (rule 5; owner decision for factory calibration).
