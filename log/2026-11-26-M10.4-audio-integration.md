# 2026-11-26 M10.4 audio integration

Ticket: `tasks/M10.4-audio-integration.md`. Depends on M10.3, closed 2026-11-26
(`log/2026-11-22-M10.3-tfa9872.md`).

## Starting point
- Kernel audio stack on WIP `a50-m10-b2-wip` `5f22a49c5`:
  - ABOX, RT5665, TFA9872 with the factory calibration gate, high-res
    timers, tfadsp.bin.
  - Last phone-tested images: B2b5 (speaker audible) / B2m.
- ADR-0005 still applies on every 6.18 boot: a per-boot read-only overlay
  of `/etc/pulse/touch.pa` without `module-droid-discover`, so PulseAudio
  has a null sink. The M5 `a50-audio-route` mask is also still active.
- M10.4 removes both and runs PulseAudio on `module-droid-discover`, which
  talks to the Android audio HAL over hwbinder.
- On 6.18, `vendor.audio-hal` crash-loops: `received signal 11` every
  ~5 s in the B2b/B2b5 kernel logs, also in M7.5. No backtrace was ever
  captured. M7.5 showed PulseAudio blocking in startup while the HAL is
  down.
- What the HAL needs (M10.1 contract): the 32-bit
  `audio.primary.exynos9610.so`, standard ALSA PCM/CTL ioctls,
  `controlC0` held while idle. Stock `/dev/snd` has `comprC0D5`
  (compress offload) and hwdep `hwC0D3`, `hwC0D9` besides the PCMs.
  Earlier 6.18 comparisons covered PCMs and controls only. Four
  HAL-relevant control names are absent on 6.18: BT SCO MIC/SPK Enable,
  `Debug Buffer Switch`, `Disable NG2`.

## C1 prep (host only)
- Read-only capture on the unchanged B2b5 images (copied as m104c1, same
  hashes).
- `tools/m10/collect_m104c1.sh` reads at ~150 s:
  - `/dev/snd`, `/proc/asound/{devices,pcm}`;
  - in the container: HAL init state and pids, its rc, its `/dev/snd`
    view, `logcat -b crash`, the newest tombstone (first 160 lines), and
    audio-related main/system logcat;
  - kernel lines for the HAL and audio.
- No mixer, PulseAudio or HAL change.
- `tools/m10/run_m104c1.sh` (standard cycle, calibration check, stock
  restore), `reference/m10-audio/m104c1-inputs.json`, 32 hashes.
- Goal: the HAL's backtrace, to decide the one fix for the next boot.
