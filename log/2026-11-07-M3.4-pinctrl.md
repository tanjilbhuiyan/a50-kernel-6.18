- driver data: 6 controllers in order ALIVE, CMGP, DISPAUD, FSYS, TOP, SHUB; banks etc0 gpa0-2 gpq0 | gpm0-25 | gpb0-2 |
  gpf0-2 | gpp0-2 gpc0-2 gpg0-4 | gph0-1. The driver matches DT nodes to data by alias pinctrlN -> aliases pinctrl0..5.
- stock DT (reference/stock-dt/dt.dtb.0.dts:11,98,660,790,1023,1467): addresses 0x11850000, 0x11c20000, 0x14a60000,
  0x13490000, 0x139b0000, 0x11080000; controller SPIs FSYS 0x96, TOP 0x10a, SHUB 0x74 (DISPAUD none); wake-up SPIs gpa0 0-7,
  gpa1 8-15, gpa2 16-23, gpm0..: 0x8e 0x8f 0x90 0x91 - - 0xa0 0xa1 0xa2 0xaa - - 0xad 0xae 0xb9 - 0xc5 0xe2 0xe3 0xe4 - - 0x110

- Baseline 4.14 binds all six controllers (/sys/bus/platform/devices/*.pinctrl -> samsung-pinctrl), incl. SHUB 11080000, so
  all register blocks are accessible from the AP.
- Layout per exynos850-pinctrl.dtsi; wakeup node compatible "samsung,exynos9610-wakeup-eint", exynos850, exynos7.
