## Result

- **Shown:** after `gout_cmu_dispaud_disp` is enabled and the probe runs at about 0.17 s, the kernel and Android userspace
  keep running to the 431 s timed dump without any logged fault in the captured window. Enabling the clock and the
  read-only reads didn't stall the system.
- **Not shown:** the probe's register values. They are not in the dump window, and USB never came up to read them.
- **USB absence: cause unverified.** Candidates:
  1. the D0b clock change. Unlikely by design (the USB CMU has its own TOP feeds) but not excluded;
  2. the host side, since this was the first run on the office laptop setup (Download mode over the same cable worked);
  3. an unrelated runtime issue.

  Telling them apart needs a control.
