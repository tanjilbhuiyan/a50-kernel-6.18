## D3a: attach with a boot-framebuffer identity map

- **Change compared with D2b:** kernel commit `17927bb22` on `a50-m72-d3a-test`
  ([patch](../reference/m72-display/0008-TEST-ONLY-attach-the-A50-display-to-the-DPU-SysMMU-wi.patch)).
  - **DT:** `iommus = <&sysmmu_dpu>` on `decon_f`, and `iommu-addresses = <&decon_f 0x0 0xca000000 0xa00000>` on the reserved
    boot framebuffer. The range equals its `reg`, so `of_iommu_get_resv_regions()` makes it `IOMMU_RESV_DIRECT` and the core
    identity-maps it in the default domain. This matches 4.14 `iovmm_map_oto()` (`dsim_drv.c:1134`).
  - **Driver:** a probe-time log only.
  - The decompiled DT differs from the flashed D2 DT only by these two properties and phandle renumbering.
- **Images:** BOOT `src/m72/boot-d3a.img`, sha256 `ca21eb022477c51a238ac0a1d6f7866ed2dc8acecbbdb35bdbbd84fcd80231c4`.
  DT `src/m72/dt-d3a.img` (round-trip identical). Inputs in [d3a-inputs.json](../reference/m72-display/d3a-inputs.json).
- **Result** ([live](../reference/m72-display/d3a-live.txt)):
  - `Attached IOMMU with pgtable 0x0000000881718000`, domain type `0x3` (DMA).
  - `iova 0xca000000 -> 0xca000000` and `0xca9a3f7c -> 0xca9a3f7c`.
  - **But `MMU_CTRL=0x00000000`:** translation was not enabled, so DMA still bypassed the SysMMU.
  - `fb_test` and `winconfig_test` passed, and the owner saw both patterns, but in bypass.
  - The core warned `late IOMMU probe at driver bind` with a WARNING backtrace from `__iommu_probe_device` (`iommu.c:612`).
    The root-level node is populated before the SysMMU registers, so the attach happens at driver bind instead.
    Non-fatal; recorded.
- **Cause:** exynos-iommu enables translation (`__sysmmu_enable`) only while the SysMMU is runtime-PM active, and ties it
  to the master with `DL_FLAG_PM_RUNTIME` (`exynos-iommu.c:1415`, `exynos_sysmmu_resume`). `a50-decon` had no runtime PM.
- **Restore:** passed; fsck clean, 6524 files ([run](../reference/m72-display/d3a-run.txt), [fsck](../reference/m72-display/d3a-baseline-fsck.txt)).

## D3a2: runtime PM enables translation, PASS

- **The single variable:** commit `b22b17c94` on `a50-m72-d3a2-test`
  ([patch](../reference/m72-display/0009-TEST-ONLY-a50-decon-keeps-runtime-PM-active-so-the-DP.patch)).
  `devm_pm_runtime_enable()` plus `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` at probe, held from then on. The log adds `MMU_CTRL`,
  `PT_BASE` and `CAPA1`, registers the SysMMU driver itself reads. The DT is identical to D3a.
- **Image:** BOOT `src/m72/boot-d3a2.img`, sha256 `312ae8beba6461dc41addb8a70c156ea3b8da83f6cb3f4e0a84d861712cddcb2`.
  Inputs in [d3a2-inputs.json](../reference/m72-display/d3a2-inputs.json).
- **Result** ([live](../reference/m72-display/d3a2-live.txt)):
  - `exynos-sysmmu 14820000.iommu: restoring state`, then `MMU_CTRL=0x00000005` (`CTRL_ENABLE`).
  - `PT_BASE=0x00881a8c` matches `Attached IOMMU with pgtable 0x0000000881a8c000`.
  - `rpm_active=1`; identity translation as in D3a.
  - Zero SysMMU faults after boot, after `fb_test` (pan; 61 vsync notifications/s) and after `winconfig_test` (all three
    cases).
  - **Owner:** "saw both patterns". The pan frame and the WIN_CONFIG frame were both fetched by IDMA0 through translation.
  - Late log at 414.7 s: `frames req=4 done=5 … timeouts=0 extra=0 te=24844 … win_configs=3 copied=2 skipped=0
    acq_timeouts=0 frame_wait_timeouts=0`. No fault lines in the dump window ([dump](../reference/m72-display/d3a2-dump.txt)).
- **Restore:** post-flight passed; fsck clean, 6518 files ([run](../reference/m72-display/d3a2-run.txt), [fsck](../reference/m72-display/d3a2-baseline-fsck.txt)).
