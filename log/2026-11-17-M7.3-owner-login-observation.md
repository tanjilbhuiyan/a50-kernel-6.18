After G2g the owner clarified that 6.18 takes a noticeable time to reach
Ubuntu's login screen and they could not log in. On clarification, the
screen did not respond to touch; PIN entry/authentication was not tested.
The prior recognizable/correct UI observations refer to login/greeter
rendering, not a verified logged-in desktop or interactive phone.

Reference config reference/m73-gpu/g2.config has CONFIG_INPUT_EVDEV and
CONFIG_INPUT_TOUCHSCREEN unset. Input-controller support remains M8 in
docs/05-plan.md. This supports treating touch as unimplemented input;
it does not determine all causes of login/session usability. Slow startup
is an owner observation without a new duration measurement; earlier
captures show late hardware-service settling and user-session activity,
but a causal timing analysis is still pending integration work.

This feedback is recorded while continuing M7.3 focused memory ABI tests.
