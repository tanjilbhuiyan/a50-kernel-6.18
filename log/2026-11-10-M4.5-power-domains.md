# 2026-11-10 M4.5 Power domains

## Decision (owner, 2026-11-10): read-only reporting in M4
All 8 stock domains (dispaud, cam, isp, vipx1, vipx2, g2d, g3d, mfc) carry need_smc in the stock DT, so switching them needs secure-firmware calls that mainline exynos-pm-domains does not make; CONFIG_PM is off and UFS hibern8 tables are unported. Control is deferred to M12.
Baseline (live 4.14, pm_genpd_summary): dispaud on; mfc, g3d, g2d, vipx2, vipx1, isp, cam off (reference/m45-pd/baseline-genpd.txt). Register map: 4.14 pmucal_local_exynos9610.h (STATUS = CONFIGURATION + 4; 0xF on, 0 off).
