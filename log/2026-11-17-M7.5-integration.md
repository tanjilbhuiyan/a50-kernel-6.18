**How Lomiri reports readiness** (disassembly `src/m75/lomiri.disasm` of the phone's `/usr/bin/lomiri`): `main()` calls
`QTimer::singleShot(0, slot)` at `0xb698` immediately before `QGuiApplication::exec()` at `0xb69c`; the slot at `0xdc20`
calls `sd_notify(0, "READY=1")` (`0xdc4c`) then `qunsetenv("NOTIFY_SOCKET")`. READY is therefore sent on the first
event-loop iteration. The notification would be accepted: the unit is `Type=notify`, `NotifyAccess=main`, `MainPID=4329`,
and `/proc/4329/exe` is `/usr/bin/lomiri` with `NOTIFY_SOCKET` set (I0 live, 110 s snapshot). So the main thread does
not reach the event loop within 120 s. It is slow, not hung: it keeps logging startup lines with gaps of 25 s
(92.0→117.0 s, the D-Bus default synchronous-call timeout), 38 s and 50 s, and prints QML startup warnings after SIGTERM.

## I1: where session startup blocks (Claude Code, 2026-11-18)

Owner: "we are in home network. check connectivity. present, flash I1". Same I0 image/DT (all 67 inherited input
hashes identical to I0r); only the collector changed (`tools/m75/collect_i1.sh`, runner `tools/m75/run_i1.sh`, inputs
`reference/m75-integration/i1-inputs.json`). Backup hash rechecked. Flash 00:13:11; restore 00:21:06; baseline up
00:21:21; post-flight passed; fsck clean 7058/3440640 files; no fault signatures in the dump. gdb is not installed on
the rootfs, so there are no user-space backtraces.

- **Android audio HAL crash-loops:** `init.svc.vendor.audio-hal=[restarting]` (pid 1426) at 120 s.
- **PulseAudio (pid 1997) never finishes startup:** main thread in `futex_wait`, `/etc/pulse/touch.pa` still open (fd 9),
  thread `HwBinder:1997_1` in `binder_ioctl_write_read` with `/dev/binderfs/hwbinder` open. `pactl info` times out (10 s)
  at 71 s and 97 s.
- **Telephony handler (pid 2710):** activated by D-Bus at 39.6 s; one thread, in `ppoll` on 3 fds, holding two sockets
  and a pipe pair; it never owns its bus names (`busctl --user list`) and is gone by 150 s (90 s start timeout). Lomiri
  logs `Failed to refresh the properties from the handler` at 117.0 s, as in I0/I0r.

Conclusion: audio HAL restarting → PulseAudio blocked in startup → telephony handler never ready → Lomiri late READY →
systemd stops it. The handler→PulseAudio link is strongly indicated (pipe pair = PulseAudio client wakeup pipe; PulseAudio
not answering clients) but not proven: socket peers can't be mapped without `CONFIG_UNIX_DIAG` and no backtrace was
possible. The root of the chain is the M10 audio stack.

## I2: ADR-0005 audio deferral (Claude Code, 2026-11-18)

Owner: "present, flash I2" (home network). I0 kernel source/config/DTB; the only change is the embedded runtime
(`tools/m75/make_i2_runtime.py`, `reference/m75-integration/i2-init-delta.patch`): `/init` +19 lines, cpio = I0 + 3

Flash 00:34:51; post-flight passed; fsck clean 7055/3440640 files; no fault signatures in the dump.

- **Deferral works:** `a50-defer-audio.service` active (exit 0) at 32.0 s; `/etc/pulse/touch.pa` is the read-only
  overlay (sha256 `275b1ea6…`); `pulseaudio.service` **active at 35.8 s** (stock 17.0 s; I0/I1 never); `pactl info`
  answers in 0.09-0.11 s; sinks: `sink.fake.sco` (null) only.
- **The session still fails:** `lomiri-telephony-service-handler` started 41.1 s, `Result=timeout`; Lomiri (PID 4795,
  start 91.0 s) `Result=timeout`, deactivating at 260 s; the restarted Lomiri fails at 312 s; dbus activation timeouts at
  160.8-161.2 s as before.
- **Correction to I1:** the handler is still one thread in `ppoll` (3 fds; fds 5/6 sockets, 8/9 pipes) with PulseAudio
  working. On stock (read-only, as phablet) the running handler has four threads: main, `QDBusConnection`, `QThread`,
  `threaded-ml` (PulseAudio's client thread). On 6.18 it therefore blocks early in startup, before its D-Bus
  connection and before any PulseAudio client exists. I1's "handler waits on PulseAudio" link is disproven. PulseAudio's
  own blocking on the audio HAL was real, and ADR-0005 fixes that; it is not what stalls the handler.

Next: find the handler's early blocking point (I3: same I2 image; collector-only additions: the handler's loaded
libraries, decoded `ppoll` fd set, unix socket paths, environment, and a manual run of the handler with Qt/Mir/Wayland
debug output under a timeout).
